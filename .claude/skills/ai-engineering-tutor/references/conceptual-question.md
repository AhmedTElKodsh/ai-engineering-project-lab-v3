# Direct question

Answer the question before asking for practice. Explain the mechanism, the specific misconception and what observation distinguishes the alternatives. Preserve exact code semantics rather than replacing key operations with vague analogies.

For example, indexing choices[0] selects the first list member; the next attribute belongs to that member, not the whole list. Supported completion counts depend on the provider. Do not ask the learner to discover undocumented behavior by causing a paid failure.

Use a small sketch or example only when it clarifies. A question about a later topic can receive a useful conceptual answer without turning the session into a full detour. Evidence genuinely demonstrated can be recorded against an appropriate capability but does not automatically complete unmet prerequisites.

Reconnect to the current pending action without imposing a quiz after every short explanation. Asking why is not a failed assessment or a reason to downgrade a skill.
